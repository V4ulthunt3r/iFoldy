The hinge

The phone folds. So does the wallet.

No extra button, no virtual flip. iFoldy reads the Duo’s own hinge: open the phone and you look straight into the wallet; close it and the leather cover is waiting on the outside.

  • Two leaves, one spine

    The inner display becomes a book-shaped wallet with pockets on both leaves and slip sleeves along the fold.

  • A cover on the outside

    Folded, the outer display is the closed wallet — with your own name and logo burned into the leather, if you like. To open it, open the phone.

  • Nothing gets lost in the fold

    Your cards, their pockets and the selected card survive every change of display. A card in your hand slides home before the phone closes.

Cards

Pockets that behave like pockets.

Cards sit in stitched slots, stack behind each other and press faint corners through the leather. Hold a stack to fan it, pull the one you want, turn it in your hand — then slide it back where it lives.

  • Scan front and back

    Hold a card steady and iFoldy captures it automatically, straightens the edges and reads the text — all on the device.

  • Masked before it’s saved

    You cover card numbers and security codes with opaque masks and confirm both sides. Only the masked images are stored.

  • Three to eight pockets

    Classic, Cascade, Offset or four, six and eight pockets across both leaves. Drag cards between slots to arrange the wallet your way.

  • Cards that open apps

    Give a card one or more links: pick from 18 banking and health apps, paste your own app link or name a Shortcut. iFoldy explains the Shortcut setup step by step and copies a ready prompt for the Shortcuts app.

  • Flip, tilt, put away

    Drag sideways to rotate a card, flick to flip it, pull down on the lower edge to return it. Every motion can be caught mid-way.

The spine sleeves

Everything else you carry.

Along the fold, labelled slip pockets hold folded paper, a few photos, your receipts and your stamp cards. Pull one out and it unfolds along its creases — into a note you can write on, a map that finds your way, or the week ahead. On the Duo, the map spreads across one leaf and the route planner sits on the other.

Business cards

Your contacts as a little stack on six paper designs. Search, flip, then call, message or mail. The cards age with your wallet.

Receipts

Scan receipts with the document camera, multi-page and straightened. Search finds the text on every page; share a receipt as one PDF.

Photos

A small stack of instant prints with a handwritten caption. Pick a photo, crop it square, swipe through the stack. Your originals stay untouched.

Pocket calendar

A four-section paper calendar shows your week from Calendar. Tap an event’s place and the paper map opens with it.

Pick what you carry Pro

Switch Notes & Reminders, Business cards, Photos, Receipts, Map, Calendar and Food & Loyalty on or off. Hidden pockets keep their contents.

Quiet Foley

Privacy

What’s in your wallet stays in your wallet.

iFoldy has no account, no cloud of its own, no analytics and no ads. Your cards, notes, photos and receipts never leave the device.

Protected on the device

Card images, receipts and photo copies use iOS’s strongest file protection and are excluded from backups.

Recognition without a server

Camera, edge detection and text recognition run with Apple’s on-device frameworks. No external AI.

Access only when you ask

Camera, Contacts, Calendar, Reminders and Location are requested the moment you use that feature. Photos come only through the system picker.

No tracking at all

No third-party SDKs. iFoldy Pro is bought through the App Store, with no purchase server of our own. Details in the privacy policy.
