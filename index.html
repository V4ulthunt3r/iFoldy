The hinge

The phone folds. So does the wallet.

No extra button, no virtual flip. iFoldy reads the Duo’s own hinge: open the phone and you look straight into the wallet; close it and the leather cover is waiting on the outside.

  • Two leaves, one spine

    The inner display becomes a book-shaped wallet with pockets on both leaves and slip sleeves along the fold.

  • A cover on the outside

    Folded, the cover display shows the front of the wallet — tap or swipe it to open a single-screen card holder.

  • Nothing gets lost in the fold

    Your cards, their pockets and the selected card survive every change of display. A card in your hand slides home before the phone closes.

Cards

Pockets that behave like pockets.

Cards sit in stitched slots, stack behind each other and press faint corners through the leather. Hold a stack to fan it, pull the one you want, turn it in your hand — then slide it back where it lives.

  • Scan front and back

    Hold a card steady and iFoldy captures it automatically, straightens the edges and reads the text — all on the device.

  • Masked before it’s saved

    You cover card numbers and security codes with opaque masks and confirm both sides. Only the masked images are stored.

  • Three to eight pockets

    Classic, Cascade, Offset or four, six and eight pockets across both leaves. Drag cards between slots to arrange the wallet your way.

  • Flip, tilt, put away

    Drag sideways to rotate a card, flick to flip it, pull down on the lower edge to return it. Every motion can be caught mid-way.

The spine sleeves

A note, a map, a little calendar.

Along the fold, slip pockets hold folded paper. Pull it out and it unfolds along its creases — into a note you can write on, a map that finds your way, or the week ahead. On the Duo, the map spreads across one leaf and the route planner sits on the other.

Details

Small things you can feel.

Book or clamshell

Let iFoldy pick the fold from the display, or choose side-by-side leaves or a top-and-bottom spread yourself.

Quiet Foley

Soft opening and closing sounds for leather, suede, canvas and carbon. They respect Silent mode.

Haptic contact

A light tap when the cover lands, a card seats or a pocket takes a card. Release speed carries into every motion.

Retaining band

An optional elastic, nylon or leather band in six colours peels over the edge before the cover opens.

Business cards

A small stack in the sleeve shows your contacts on six paper designs — with call, message and mail at hand.

For everyone

VoiceOver labels on every fitting, Reduce Motion support, and an interface in English and German.

Privacy

What’s in your wallet stays in your wallet.

iFoldy has no account, no cloud of its own, no analytics and no ads. Your cards and notes never leave the device.

Protected on the device

Card images use iOS’s strongest file protection and are excluded from backups.

Recognition without a server

Camera, edge detection and text recognition run with Apple’s on-device frameworks. No external AI.

Access only when you ask

Camera, Contacts, Calendar, Reminders and Location are requested the moment you use that feature — not before.

No tracking at all

No third-party SDKs. Details in the privacy policy.
